# Hot Takes Exchange

A multiplayer debate game where players back a side with fictional credits, discuss the hot take, and vote on the winner.

## How to play

1. Join the room with a nickname.
2. Choose AGREE or DISAGREE and lock in your credit prediction.
3. Debate the take, then cast your final vote.
4. Earn credits for correct predictions and check the leaderboard.

The host moves the game between rounds. Players can also submit hot takes for the host to review. All credits are fictional.
