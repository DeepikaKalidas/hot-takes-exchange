🌶 THE QUICK RULES

How to play Hot Takes Exchange

HR said we need team bonding. Here is how this works:

  1. Back a side. During the 2-minute market, invest 100 to 500 fictional credits on AGREE or DISAGREE. Your stake is locked for the round.
  2. Make your case. Debate lasts 3 minutes. React to the room and optionally vote once for which side argued better.
  3. Cast your final vote. During the 1-minute vote, choose AGREE or DISAGREE once. This decides the winner.
  4. Earn credits. A correct prediction pays the displayed odds; a wrong one loses its stake. A tie returns your stake. Credits are just for fun.
  5. Submit a hot take. Between rounds, spend 150 credits to send an anonymous take. The host reviews it and may queue it for a future round.

Phases advance automatically when their timers end, and the host can advance early. Your market pick stays private until results.
